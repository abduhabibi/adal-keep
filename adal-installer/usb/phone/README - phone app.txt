Adal Keep on the phone (Android)
================================

Install - any one of these:
  * Send "Adal Keep.apk" to the phone with Xender (or Telegram, a cable...),
    open it on the phone and press Install. The phone may ask to allow
    installing apps from Xender / Files: allow it once.
  * Plug the phone into a PC with a USB cable and double-click
    "Install on a phone (USB cable).bat" (needs "USB debugging" on the phone;
    the window explains how).

First time - on the office Wi-Fi (the same Wi-Fi as the main PC):
  1. Open Adal Keep on the phone.
  2. Type the PC's 5-digit number. Adal Keep on the main PC shows it in
     Settings > Main PC ("Phone app - this PC's number").
  3. Choose your name on the Employees page, like on a PC.

Away from the office:
  Install the Tailscale app on the phone once and sign in to the same
  Tailscale account as the main PC. With Tailscale on, Adal Keep opens from
  anywhere.

When a run needs you (captcha, code, Submit), the phone rings while Adal Keep
is open. Open her profile > AI actions: the PC's tab shows live - tap on it
and type there.

A new "Adal Keep.apk" installs over the old one; nothing is lost.
