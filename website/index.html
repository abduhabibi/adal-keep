How it works

From installer to autopilot in three steps

Adal Keep installs as a desktop app, runs locally on your machine, and starts working the moment files land in your folders.

01

Install & connect

Run the installer, lock the license to your machine, and the app boots its own backend, file watcher and companion agent automatically.

02

Capture & organise

Save passports, slips and photos as you normally would. Adal Keep detects, encrypts, OCRs and attaches each file to the right candidate.

03

Automate & submit

The AI decides each candidate's next action, drafts CVs, drives the portals, and hands you only the steps that truly need a human.

01 · THE PROBLEM

Massive data, slow portals

Candidate files scattered across disconnected systems, sluggish portals and 100% manual repetition — with crashes that lose work mid-session.

02 · ARCHITECTURE

Visible browser automation

A local desktop app drives a real, visible Brave session over Chrome DevTools Protocol on port 9222, so the operator watches every action live.

03 · PIPELINE

Ingest & deep-vision sort

Chokidar watches your inbox with a 3-second write-settle, DeepSeek Vision classifies 22 document types, and Sharp splits merged passport/photo scans.

04 · WORKFLOWS

LMIS & Musaned

Police certificates, CV making and Saudi Musaned/Tawtheeq submissions — around 90% automated, with a graceful handoff for OTP and captcha.

05 · PRINCIPLES

Determinism over AI hype

Action → Read State → Compare → Retry loops beat LLMs on speed and cost. AI is reserved for genuine ambiguity, never the happy path.

06 · SAFEGUARDS

Human-in-the-loop

Consequential legal filings and reCAPTCHAs stay strictly human-approved, while the Jev decision layer scores risk after submission.

07 · BY THE NUMBERS

Production-grade scale

37 services & 31 routes, 25 pages & components, 3 dedicated lanes on 5-second polling, and 90% automation of manual entry.

08 · ROADMAP

From desktop to SaaS

Vision-AI profile JSON and WhatsApp/SMS triggers come next, then the hardware-locked single-tenant app evolves into managed multi-tenant SaaS.

Questions

Frequently asked

Everything you need to know before you install Adal Keep.

Does Adal Keep need an internet connection?+
No. Adal Keep runs entirely on your own machine and local network. The AI assistant and portal automations only reach the internet when you use those specific features.
Where is my data stored?+
In a local database on your computer, plus an encrypted file store. Nothing leaves your machine unless you explicitly send it to a portal or the AI provider.
How does licensing work?+
The license is locked to your hardware fingerprint. Trials run for 14 days, then switch to read-only until you enter an access code — your data is never deleted.
Which portals are supported?+
The LMIS police-certificate workflow, Saudi Musaned/Tawtheeq, and Pextran — with human-in-the-loop checkpoints for any step that needs hands-on input.
Can several people use it at once?+
Yes. Adal Keep ships with roles, branches and tenant isolation, so a whole team — or several branches — can work from one deployment.
