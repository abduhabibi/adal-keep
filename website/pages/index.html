How to install

About 15 minutes. Use the PC that will stay on in the office (the main PC) first.

  1. Download the installer above and double-click Install Adal Keep. Say Yes if Windows asks.
  2. Type 1 for the Main PC (it holds your data), or 2 for a Companion (an employee PC). Wait until it says it is finished.
  3. Adal Keep opens. Type your agency name, phone and e-mail once. That is all you have to do.
  4. Adal sets up your agency from far away — templates, e-mail, sign-in numbers, staff. You are told when it is Ready.
  5. One person from Adal Keep comes to train your staff.
